# Meet the Builders — Short Video Script

Target length: 65–75 seconds

Replace `[YOUR NAME]` and `[CITY, COUNTRY]` before recording.

## Narration and Screen Flow

### 0:00–0:08 — Builder introduction

**Screen:** Face camera, followed by the project title.

"Hi, I'm [YOUR NAME], a builder from [CITY, COUNTRY]. I built this project for
the Google Cloud Gen AI Academy APAC Edition on Hack2Skill and Meet the
Builders."

### 0:08–0:18 — The problem

**Screen:** Quickly show separate task, note, and calendar screens or simple
icons.

"Across Asia-Pacific, students, professionals, and small teams manage work
across disconnected tools. Tasks get missed, notes become hard to find, and
understanding progress takes extra effort."

### 0:18–0:48 — The solution and live demonstration

**Screen:** Open the Multi-Agent Productivity Assistant and use these prompts.
Cut directly to each formatted result.

**Task prompt:**

> Create a high-priority task titled "Submit weekly report" due on 2026-07-24.

**Semantic note-search prompt:**

> Search my notes for ideas about reducing distractions and doing focused work.

**Calendar prompt:**

> Schedule a 30-minute weekly planning session on 2026-07-23 at 16:00.

**Analytics prompt:**

> Show my task completion rate by priority and summarize today's activity.

**Narration:**

"I built the Multi-Agent Productivity Assistant: one conversation for managing
tasks, finding notes by meaning, scheduling events, and viewing live insights.
A coordinator routes each request to the right specialist."

### 0:48–1:03 — Google AI and Cloud technology

**Screen:** Show the architecture diagram, then a quick montage of Vertex AI,
Cloud Run, AlloyDB, and BigQuery in the Google Cloud console.

"Gemini 2.5 Flash on Vertex AI and Google Agent Development Kit power the
experience. AlloyDB AI enables semantic search, Cloud Run hosts the application,
and BigQuery provides live analytics—all protected by IAM, Secret Manager, and
private networking."

### 1:03–1:12 — Impact and closing

**Screen:** Return to face camera or the working assistant. Display the project
name and repository link.

"This solution helps people spend less time organizing and more time completing
meaningful work. I'm sharing it through Meet the Builders—and inviting APAC
builders to share theirs."

## Recording Checklist

- Use a vertical or square-safe composition if the video will also be posted on LinkedIn.
- Keep prompts prepared for copy and paste; remove loading pauses during editing.
- Use only synthetic demo records and never reveal secrets, tokens, or raw headers.
- Show each result for two to three seconds and zoom in enough for mobile viewers.
- Add captions for every narration line and keep background music below the voice.
- End with: project name, `Google Cloud Gen AI Academy APAC Edition`,
  `Meet the Builders`, and the repository or project link.

## Suggested Title

**Meet the Builders: A Multi-Agent Productivity Assistant Built with Google AI**
